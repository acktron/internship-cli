To all the chefs out there!The most dangerous thought a chef can have is: “There’s nothing left for me to learn.”Especially when you already have dozens of successful openings behind you, a strong CV, and many years of experience.Before launching your own project, I would always recommend becoming a student again, even if just for a little while.Do an internship. Step into someone else’s kitchen. See how other people work. Don’t teach, don’t explain how things should be done — just observe and absorb.Because sometimes a few weeks in someone else’s kitchen can give you more new ideas than several years in your own.We get used to our own processes, our own products, and our own ways of doing things very quickly.But it’s always better to remain a bit of a student.
